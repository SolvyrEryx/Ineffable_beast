Who is IneffableBeast?

IneffableBeast is a Bengaluru-based AI product engineer and competitive Minecraft player who represented India twice at the BedWars World Cup. He studies artificial intelligence and data science, contributes to research on AI and creativity, and works with startups in Italy and Mumbai.

AI, machine learning, and product

He is pursuing an engineering degree in AI and data science. His research interests center on how machine learning can support creative work, including how people use AI to explore ideas and how to evaluate whether a tool actually improves their workflow. He has contributed to research connected with international conferences.

As an AI product engineer, he has worked with multiple startups in Italy and Mumbai. His interests span the work that happens around a model: choosing a problem worth solving, prototyping the experience, evaluating the output, and building a product people can use. On the marketing side, he is interested in positioning AI products, explaining them through demos, and using audience feedback to sharpen the message.

Competitive Minecraft

Two BedWars World Cup appearances for India, a 300+ BedWars winstreak, and a solo win on Deadwood in 2:05.250. The verified Speedrun.com submission lists the run sixth in Hypixel BedWars Solo Win, No DC, and includes the run video.

His BedWars accounts include o revil, which hit a 300 FKDR, and WhatsFKDR, which hit 150 FKDR. FKDR is the ratio of final kills to final deaths. He also played under IneffableBeast and F_u_, reached Bridge Grandmaster on multiple accounts, and played PotPvP and Boxing on Minemen Asia.

He also ran and later sold AnticheatArena, his own Minecraft server.

PC setup and recording

IneffableBeast’s gaming and recording PC has an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060, an Intel Core i5-12400F, and 16 GB of RAM. His setup also includes a 240 Hz monitor, a Glorious Model O Pro mouse, and a mechanical keyboard.

He captures gameplay at 1440p and 240 fps and pays close attention to render and export settings so fast fights stay clear and motion looks smooth in the finished edit. His YouTube gameplay videos show the finished work.
